About Sajvera Capital

Agriculture-Focused Investment Platform

Sajvera Capital is an agriculture-focused investment platform powered by PureOre Capital, created to connect people with agricultural opportunities while supporting the development and expansion of farming activities.

Official Release: 15 September 2026

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to encourage greater participation in agriculture by connecting people with opportunities within the farming sector.

We believe agriculture can create livelihoods, support communities, develop businesses, strengthen food production, and contribute to economic development.

Our Agricultural Focus

Sajvera Capital is built around agricultural opportunities including:

  • Crop farming
  • Vegetable production
  • Maize farming
  • Livestock
  • Other commercial agricultural activities

By bringing agriculture and digital technology together, Sajvera Capital aims to make agricultural participation more accessible, organized, and convenient for its members.

Who We Support

Sajvera Capital is designed to support different types of agricultural participants:

  • People who want to start their journey into farming.
  • Existing farmers looking to expand their agricultural activities.
  • Individuals interested in participating in agricultural projects.
  • Members who want to support productive farming activities through available investment opportunities.
  • Agricultural entrepreneurs working toward larger commercial farming operations.
  • People who want to learn more about agriculture and explore opportunities within the farming sector.
Our Regional Presence

Sajvera Capital is designed to serve members across multiple African markets, with an initial focus on:

  • Zambia
  • Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC)
  • Tanzania
  • Zimbabwe
  • Ghana

These countries represent important agricultural markets with strong farming communities, agricultural entrepreneurs, and opportunities across crop production, livestock, vegetables, maize, and other areas of commercial agriculture.

Sajvera Capital aims to build a connected community where people from different countries can participate in agricultural opportunities, learn from one another, support farming initiatives, and explore ways to become more involved in the agricultural sector.

Regional Agricultural Community

Our goal is to bring together farmers, agricultural entrepreneurs, aspiring farmers, and people interested in supporting agriculture from across these countries.

Each country has its own agricultural strengths, farming communities, and opportunities. Through Sajvera Capital, we aim to create a platform that recognizes these differences while building a broader regional agricultural community.

Zambia • DRC • Tanzania • Zimbabwe • Ghana

As Sajvera Capital grows, the platform may expand its reach to additional African markets and agricultural communities.

About Our Development

Sajvera Capital is scheduled for its official release on 15 September 2026 and is powered by PureOre Capital, which began on 13 February 2025.

Sajvera Capital is being developed with agriculture at its center, with the aim of supporting people who want to become farmers, helping existing agricultural participants grow their activities, and providing opportunities for members to participate in agricultural projects through the platform.
