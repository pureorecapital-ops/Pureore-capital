Effective date: May 26, 2025

Pureore Capital ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our investment platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and encrypted password.
  • Financial data: deposit/withdrawal records, transaction history, wallet balances.
  • Technical data: IP address, device type, browser version, and usage logs.
  • Communications: messages you send to our support team.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To process deposits, withdrawals, and investment requests.
  • To prevent fraud, money laundering, and unauthorised access.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with:

  • Service providers (payment processors, cloud hosting) under strict confidentiality.
  • Law enforcement if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

4. Data Security

We use industry‑standard encryption (SSL/TLS), firewalls, and access controls. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential.

5. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data. Contact us at support@pureorecapital.com (placeholder). You may also withdraw consent for marketing communications.
