Frequently Asked Questions
How do I make a deposit?
To make a deposit, go to the Profile page and tap "Deposit". Follow the on-screen instructions to enter the amount and your payment phone number, then complete the payment using the provided agent details.
How do I withdraw funds?
To withdraw funds, go to the Profile page and tap "Withdraw". Ensure you have a saved withdrawal account, enter the amount, confirm the details, and submit your request. Withdrawals are processed during business hours.
What are the Daily Check rewards?
Daily Check is a 7-day reward program. Check in each day to claim the configured amount. The rewards increase as you progress through the week. You can claim one day per day, and missed days may be recoverable based on platform rules.
How do I refer someone?
Go to the Referrals page to find your unique referral code and link. Share the link with friends. When they register using your code, you'll earn referral bonuses as per the platform's rules.
What is the withdrawal fee?
A 10% withdrawal fee applies to all withdrawals. You will receive 90% of the requested amount. The fee is calculated automatically during the withdrawal process.
